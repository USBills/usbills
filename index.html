H.R. 1013 (Retirement Fairness for Charities and Educational Institutions Act of 2025) is a bill that amends 403(b) retirement plans so that they can invest in lower-cost collective investment trusts and expand their options. Those trusts feature lower overhead costs and fees which benefit anyone with a 403(b) retirement plan who is looking to invest in something else. The bill primarily benefits individuals holding a 403(b) retirement account (e.g. university faculty, healthcare workers, charity/nonprofit/church employees) by giving them the same investment parity that private workers have.

H. Res. 1650 is a simple, procedural rule resolution that establishes the floor debate rules for H.R. 1013 and enables the consideration of the bill. It is strictly functional and has allocated at least 1 hour for full debate.

H. Res. is a simple resolution introduced in the House this week by Rep. Thomas Massie (R-KY-4) to initiate the articles of impeachment against incumbent Secretary of War Pete Hegseth. It alleges that Secretary Hegseth violated the U.S. Constitution by engaging in military hostilities against Iran for more than 90 days without congressional approval and failed to cease hostilities. This resolution benefits anti-interventionist lawmakers, opponents of the Trump Administration, and critics of the Iran war.
