H.R. 9770 (Continuing Appropriations Act, 2027) is a bill that continues to fund the federal government and all three branches for the fiscal year of 2027. The bill essentially seeks to look at the funding in the fiscal year of 2026 and match the appropriations for the fiscal year of 2027. The bill also included multiple previous appropriation bills as a reference for any future funding. This bill benefits federal government employees the most as it pays their salaries and allows them to continue working.

H.R. 7008 (Stop Insider Trading Act) is a bill that aims to make it illegal for Members of Congress and their immediate families to trade on the stock market using insider information obtained in Congress. The bill requires advance notice for any stock sales along with heavy penalties if stock shares are bought. This bill benefits the average American taxpayer by reducing corruption and holding elected representatives accountable for their finances.

H.R. 6955 (Main Street Capital Access Act) is a sweeping bill that makes changes to the federal banking system and community banks. The bill lowers the barrier to entry for newly established banks by implementing a 3-year period to meet capital requirements after inception. The bill also lifts the asset limits that smaller banks use and adjusts them for inflation while also placing a 90-day limit for mergers and acquisitions, accelerating banks looking to merge. The bill also includes provisions for regulators to supervise smaller banks according to more flexible standards rather than the standards used for global institutions. Finally, the bill protects smaller banks from the Federal Reserve's climate-related stress tests. Critics of the bill have claimed that its passing into law would mean fewer consumer protections and fewer regulated safeguards implemented after the 2008 financial crisis. The bill primarily benefits new, small, rural, or community banks by making their establishments much more lax.

H.R. 8800 (National Defense Authorization Act for Fiscal Year 2027) is a bill that appropriates approximately $1.15 trillion for the Department of Defense (DoD) to spend on its military. The bill outlines pay for domestic needs, innovative priorities, and theaters of war that the U.S. military needs to heed in the current and future years. The bill also allocated funding to focus on America's international allies, especially those with which it has defense/security pacts. This bill directly benefits military servicemembers, defense contractors, and national security agencies by giving them the funding they need to operate.

H.R. 8884 (Removing Barriers to Work for Disabled Americans Act) is a bill that authorizes the Social Security Administration (SSA) to fund and conduct pilot programs for disabled Americans who want to work but are too afraid to leave the program out of fear of missing their benefits. The bill protects participants and reauthorizes the SSA until 2030 to test alternative rules for those with disabilities. This bill benefits disabled Americans who are hindered from working and leaving the Social Security Disability program by increasing work opportunities and offering financial protection.
