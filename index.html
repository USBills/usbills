H.R. 8595 (National Security, Department of State, and Related Programs Appropriations Act, 2027) is a bill that appropriates $49.8 billion for American diplomatic and foreign service means with $8.9 billion allocated to HIV/AIDS programs around the world and $3.3 billion in military aid to Israel. The bill also allocates money to counter China's global influence while simultaneously reducing money to fund the U.N. This bill directly benefits Israel, the global fight against HIV/AIDS, and foreign national security services that the U.S. currently operates.

H.R. 9022 (Energy and Water Development and Related Agencies Appropriations Act, 2027) is a bill that allocates funding to where and how the U.S. spends its money on infrastructure and energy projects for the fiscal year. The bill allocates billions to the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers and Bureau of Reclamation for flood control maintenance and water infrastructure while funding the Department of Energy (DOE) for all of its energy projects. It specifically allocates money to the Cybersecurity, Energy Security, and Emergency Response (CESER) program to protect the nation's infrastructure from physical or cyber attacks and emphasizes the integration of AI within the energy sector. The bill, through its funding and grants, benefits the energy industry, American taxpayers, conservationists, and communities most at risk from the effects of climate change.

H.R. 9237 (Take Care of America’s Veterans Act) is a comprehensive bill that compiles 60 veteran bills into a single piece of legislation aimed at overhauling the Department of Veterans Affairs (VA). Key provisions in the bill include ensuring combat veterans receive their full benefits even if they retire before 20 years of service, allowing spousal benefits to remain if the surviving spouse decides to remarry, expanding support for all disadvantaged veteran groups, cutting disability compensation, and stripping away labor rights for VA psychologists. This bill mainly benefits injured veterans, surviving spouses, and Gold Star families.

H.R. 1181 (Protecting Privacy in Purchases Act) is a bill that protects the 2nd Amendment by preventing financial surveillance by banks or credit card companies when purchasing a firearm and overrules state laws that require banks to use specific firearm merchant codes. The bill mainly benefits private gun owners and retailers by providing them with protection from financial surveillance.
