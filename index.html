July 13th, 2026 to July 16th, 2026

H.R. 139 (Sunshine Protection Act of 2025) is a bill that establishes Daylight Saving Time as the permanent standard across the entire nation. Additionally, it grants states with Daylight Saving Time exemptions the ability to remain on standard time. This bill benefits people who enjoy the outdoors and sunshine, along with commuters, the retail sector, and the business sector by permanently adding an hour of sunshine to their schedules.

H.R. 8595 (National Security, Department of State, and Related Programs Appropriations Act, 2027) is a bill that appropriates approximately $47 billion in funding for U.S. foreign policy and diplomacy. The bill significantly cuts foreign assistance under the "America First" framework and instead allocates funds to bolstering the nation's national security in countering global adversaries such as Russia, China, and Iran. This bill benefits the GOP, Pacific global allies, and the national security sector by shifting away from aid towards a more hawkish stance against nations that threaten the interests of the United States and its allies.

H.R. 9237 (Take Care of America’s Veterans Act) is a bill that incorporates over 60+ bills aimed at overhauling the Department of Veterans Affairs and its programs for the future. The reforms are massive and include nearly everything from mental health to sexual trauma to survivor benefits. To fund the bill, the VA has included controversial cuts, including changes to how tinnitus and sleep apnea are diagnosed. The bill mainly benefits combat-injured retirees, surviving spouses, and disabled veterans while impairing future disabled veterans by using their benefits currently.

H.R. 1181 (Protecting Privacy in Purchases Act) is a bill that prohibits the labeling of Merchant Category Codes for ammunitions and firearms by credit card companies and other payment networks. The bill is important because it protects legal gun-owners from financial surveillance and creates a uniform, federal standard for ammunition and firearm purchases across the nation. The bill mainly benefits proponents of the 2nd Amendment and firearm retailers by protecting their financials.
