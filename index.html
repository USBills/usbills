August 31st, 2026 to September 3rd, 2026

H.R. 9436 (Northeast Lobsterman Protection Act of 2026) is a bill that amends the Consolidated Appropriations Act to extend the legislative delays that target maritime regulations on North Atlantic whale protections. Since right whales often get entangled in commercial fishing gear, the bill aims to extend the timeline for fisheries to comply with regulations by adopting alternative gear that protects both their economic interests and the right whales. This bill benefits Northeastern regional fishing businesses along with coastal economies dependent on harvesting seafood.

H.R. 4795 (Protect Economic and Academic Freedom Act of 2026) is a bill that introduces strict compliance measures for boycotts in colleges and universities that participate in federal aid programs. The bill mandates that higher education institutions cannot engage in commercial boycotts against partner nations (specifically Israel) and requires universities to annually certify that they do not restrict students from engaging in projects, programs, or studying abroad in partner nations. This bill primarily benefits Israel along with other partner nations and students studying abroad by leveraging federal funding to protect international relations.

H.R. 1501 (Protecting Domestic Mining Act of 2025) is a bill that aims to boost domestic mining by including mining and mineral processing in the FAST Act while also blocking the Federal Permitting Improvement Steering Council (FPISC) from finalizing rules that restrict mining projects under the FAST Act. The bill is important because it accelerates the mining of rare earth minerals and secures America's supply chain from industry shocks. The bill benefits mining/resource extraction companies along with clean technology manufacturers by making it easier to obtain mining permits which produces more minerals for use.

H. Res. 1490 is a House resolution condemning socialism in all its forms along with the Democratic Socialists of America (DSA). This resolution directly benefits all who are vehemently opposed to socialism in American society.

H. Res. (H. Rept. 119-756) is a House resolution that censures Representative Chuck Edwards (NC-11) for his sexual harassment and misconduct with female staffers. It follows a report by the House Ethics Committee which found evidence of a hostile work environment.
